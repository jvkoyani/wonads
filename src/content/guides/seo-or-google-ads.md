## Compare the decisions

| Question | SEO | Google Search Ads |
|---|---|---|
| When does discovery start? | Depends on crawling, indexing, competition and the work required. | Ads can serve after setup and approval; profitable acquisition still needs testing. |
| Where does investment go? | Technical work, service content, evidence and maintenance. | Media spend, management, landing pages and tracking. |
| What happens when spend stops? | Useful pages remain, but visibility can change and work may still be needed. | Paid delivery generally stops when the budget stops. |
| What must be ready? | Useful accessible pages and an implementable content plan. | An offer, suitable destination, budget and inquiry handling. |

## A practical decision sequence

- Check that customers understand the offer and can submit an inquiry.
- Confirm whether people already search for the service.
- Decide how soon demand is needed and how much testing budget is available.
- Choose one measurable initial objective rather than launching every channel.

## Illustrative scenario

A new local service with a clear offer may test a focused search campaign while building its service pages. A business with useful content but indexing errors may need technical SEO first. These are examples of decision logic, not Won Ads client results.

## What to measure

Compare qualified inquiries and customers against total acquisition cost. Do not compare organic impressions with paid leads as if they were the same outcome. Keep media, management and implementation costs visible.
