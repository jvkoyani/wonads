## Four content roles

| Role | Example brief |
|---|---|
| Explain | Show what a service includes and who it suits. |
| Demonstrate | Annotate an approved deliverable and explain a decision. |
| Answer | Address a recurring question about scope, cost or process. |
| Invite | Offer an appropriate next step for interested customers. |

## Plan production before frequency

Agree who supplies footage, who approves claims and which formats the team can sustain. A calendar that depends on unavailable assets will drift into generic posts. Reuse one substantial explanation as a short video, annotated image and linked resource when each format adds value.

## Connect social and search

Use consistent business information and direct people to the relevant service or resource page. Customer questions in comments can improve website FAQs. Social activity supports discovery and brand familiarity; a post count is not a promise of search rankings.

## Review business usefulness

Track useful inquiries, recurring objections and relevant visits alongside reach. A post that answers a buying question may be commercially useful without becoming a high-reach entertainment piece.
