## Compare the practical requirements

| Decision | Google Search | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| Starting signal | A search query that may express a current need. | Audience and creative response to an offer. |
| Creative requirement | Clear ads aligned with the search and destination. | Strong visual and written explanations that earn attention. |
| Common mismatch | Broad or wrong-intent queries reaching an unsuitable offer. | Low-friction responses without genuine purchase readiness. |
| Useful measurement | Qualified inquiries and sales outcomes. | Qualified inquiries and sales outcomes. |

## Instant forms versus landing pages

An instant form can simplify the response step. A landing page gives space to explain a complex service, exclusions and evidence. Compare the quality of the resulting conversations, not just cost per submission.

## A fair test

Agree the offer, geography, qualification definition and follow-up responsibilities. Record spend and delivery costs. Account for different customer readiness and buying cycles before declaring a channel the winner.

## When to fix the offer first

If customers cannot understand the promise or the business cannot respond to leads, launching another channel may multiply the same problem. Clarify the service and the next step before dividing budget across platforms.
