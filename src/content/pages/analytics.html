Questions before you start

Can analytics numbers be perfectly identical?

Different platforms use different attribution and processing rules. The goal is documented, decision-useful measurement rather than forced equality.

What should count as a conversion?

A conversion should represent a meaningful business action. We separate micro-actions from enquiries, qualified leads and sales where possible.

Do you work with GA4 and Google Tag Manager?

These tools can be included along with platform pixels and website events when access and consent requirements are clear.

When should CRO begin?

CRO is most useful after the offer, traffic, measurement and conversion volume are sufficient to evaluate changes.

Will you document the setup?

Documentation and handover should be included in the agreed scope so future teams understand event names, triggers and limitations.

A practical starting point

Understand where visitors become customers

Measurement should separate interest from an accepted inquiry and a won customer. Conversion research then helps explain where suitable visitors encounter friction.

Map the events

Distinguish contact clicks, accepted submissions, qualified leads and sales. Avoid counting a button click as an email delivery or booked appointment.

Investigate conversion friction

Review message clarity, mobile layouts, form errors and offer fit. Low-traffic sites often benefit from customer feedback and usability reviews before split tests.

Interpret results carefully

Record changes to budgets, traffic sources and offers. Before-and-after differences alone cannot prove that one change caused the outcome.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • Which events are currently recorded?
  • Can leads be linked to sales?
  • Is there enough traffic for an experiment?
