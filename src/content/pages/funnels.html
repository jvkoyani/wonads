What the engagement needs to solve

Won Ads connects acquisition, landing pages, qualification, CRM routing and follow-up so a lead does not disappear between marketing and sales. The work begins by agreeing what a useful lead actually means.

Who this is for

Service and commerce businesses that can define their offer, respond consistently and share sales feedback.

Problems we address

  • High lead volume with poor fit
  • Forms collecting too little or too much
  • Slow lead assignment and response
  • Marketing reports disconnected from sales outcomes

What the scope can include

  • Offer and qualification plan
  • Channel and landing journey
  • Form, CRM and routing setup
  • Follow-up and measurement design

How performance is reviewed

Qualified leads, acceptance rate, response time, appointment rate, opportunity value and cost per accepted enquiry.

Working process

We diagnose the current position, agree the scope and responsibilities, implement in reviewable stages, validate the result and use evidence to decide the next improvement.

Before work begins

Final deliverables, access, exclusions, timelines, revision or optimisation cadence and third-party costs are confirmed in the proposal. Unsupported claims are not added.

Questions before you start

What counts as a qualified lead?

The definition is agreed from location, need, budget, timing and any business-specific acceptance rules.

Which channels do you use?

Channels are selected from demand and offer fit. Google Ads, Meta Ads, organic search or referral journeys may play different roles.

Is a CRM included?

CRM setup can be included when the platform, stages, users, fields and ownership requirements are agreed.

Who follows up with leads?

The client normally owns sales conversations. Won Ads can design routing and reminders, but responsibilities and response targets must be explicit.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

No fixed volume can be guaranteed responsibly without stable demand, budget, offer and conversion evidence.

How will lead quality be reported?

Campaign data is connected with qualification and sales-stage feedback wherever the available systems allow it.

A practical starting point

Connect acquisition, qualification and follow-up

Lead generation is a process from discovery to a useful sales conversation. Campaigns, pages, forms and follow-up need to work together, with an agreed definition of a qualified lead.

Define qualification

Agree service fit, geography, budget and buying readiness. Keep raw inquiries and qualified opportunities separate in reporting.

Reduce friction

Ask only for information needed at the current step. Show what happens after submission and make mobile forms easy to complete.

Close the feedback loop

Give every accepted inquiry an owner and stage. Use sales feedback to adjust campaigns and the offer.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • What makes a lead suitable?
  • Who handles new inquiries?
  • Where are outcomes recorded?
