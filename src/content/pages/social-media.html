Questions before you start

What is included in social media management?

The exact scope can cover planning, calendar creation, design, captions, publishing, reporting and agreed community tasks. Production volume is confirmed in the proposal.

Which platforms do you manage?

Platform choice depends on where your customers make decisions. Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn are evaluated rather than included automatically.

Does Won Ads create reels and other content?

Reels and creative production can be included. The proposal clarifies who supplies footage, filming requirements, edit rounds and approval dates.

Do you answer comments and direct messages?

Community management is available when responsibilities, response rules and escalation points are agreed in advance.

Is paid advertising included?

Organic management and paid media are separate services unless a combined scope is agreed. Advertising media spend is always separate.

How quickly can social media improve results?

A consistent publishing system can be established quickly, but audience and commercial outcomes depend on the offer, creative inputs, distribution and time.
