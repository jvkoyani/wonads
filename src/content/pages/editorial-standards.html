Useful explanations. Traceable claims.

Our publishing standard is to explain recommendations clearly and distinguish general guidance, illustrative examples and documented client outcomes.

Evidence before claims

  • Attribute third-party guidance to its original source.
  • Describe hypothetical examples as illustrations.
  • Publish client names, screenshots, logos and results only with permission.
  • State measurement periods, definitions and limitations with performance results.
  • Do not invent awards, credentials, reviews or partnerships.

Human review and assisted drafting

Tools may help organize or draft material. The business remains responsible for checking the final copy. A tool-generated draft is not evidence of experience or a substitute for checking a claim.

Corrections and dates

If you identify an inaccurate statement, email wonads.com@gmail.com with the page and correction. Publication dates identify publication; updated dates should only change after a meaningful review.
