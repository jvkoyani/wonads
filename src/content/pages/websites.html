Won Ads builds business websites and landing pages around the questions buyers need answered before they contact you. Structure, mobile usability, forms, tracking and ownership are planned alongside the visual design.

Who this is for

Businesses launching a new offer, replacing a difficult website or creating a focused landing journey for paid and organic demand.

Problems we address

  • A site that looks good but explains little
  • Mobile journeys with unclear actions
  • Forms and analytics added after launch
  • Redesigns that lose useful search URLs

What the scope can include

  • Discovery and page plan
  • Wireframes and content requirements
  • Responsive design and build
  • Form, analytics and launch QA

How performance is reviewed

Qualified form submissions, calls or messages, conversion rate, page engagement, technical performance and successful search migration.

Questions before you start

How long does a business website take?

Timing depends on page count, copy, integrations and approval speed. The proposal sets milestones after the requirements are clear.

Is website copy included?

Copy refinement or full website copy can be scoped separately. Responsibilities and revision rounds are confirmed before work begins.

Will I own the website and domain?

Domain, hosting, source access and handover terms should be documented in the proposal. Won Ads recommends client ownership of core business accounts.

Is SEO included in website development?

SEO foundations such as crawlable structure, titles, canonicals and migration checks can be included. Ongoing SEO is a separate programme.

Which platform will you use?

The platform is selected around editing needs, integrations, performance, budget and ownership rather than a default tool.

Do you build landing pages for advertising campaigns?

Yes. Landing pages can be planned with the offer, qualification fields, tracking and campaign message as one conversion journey.

A practical starting point

Business websites that explain and convert

A business website should explain what you offer, establish fit and make contact straightforward. Plan content, mobile usability and inquiry handling alongside the design.

Plan the buying journey

Map the homepage, services, proof, pricing context and contact route. Give each important page a distinct purpose instead of repeating the same pitch.

Protect existing visibility

Before a redesign, inventory useful URLs and map any necessary redirects. Check titles, canonicals, forms and navigation after launch.

Define ownership

Clarify hosting, domain access, source files, content editing, maintenance and third-party fees in the proposal.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • Is this a redesign or a new website?
  • Who supplies copy and images?
  • Which integrations and forms are needed?
