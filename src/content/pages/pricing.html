What makes a quote change?

The number of channels, website size, content requirements, tracking condition, approval process and integration complexity all affect effort. A lower fee can cover a narrower scope; compare responsibilities and outputs, not just the headline price.

Bring these details to the discussion

  • Business goal and customer profile.
  • Current website and relevant account links.
  • Locations served and inquiry-handling capacity.
  • Budget, preferred timeline and required deliverables.
