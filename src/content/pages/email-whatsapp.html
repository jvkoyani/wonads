Questions before you start

Do you provide contact lists?

No. Marketing should use legitimate contacts collected by the business with appropriate permission.

Are messaging charges included?

Platform, messaging and third-party tool charges are separate unless the proposal explicitly includes them.

Can email and WhatsApp work together?

Yes. The sequence should respect channel permission, timing and the role of each message.

Do you build abandoned-cart flows?

They can be included for suitable commerce platforms when the required events and customer permissions are available.

How do customers opt out?

Every workflow should include the appropriate unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism for the channel and platform.

A practical starting point

Useful follow-up with a clear reason to contact

Email and WhatsApp workflows should match the customer relationship and the purpose of the message. Clarify contact permissions, content approvals and opt-out handling before launch.

Separate message purposes

An inquiry reply, an appointment reminder and a promotional campaign have different expectations. Design the workflow around the actual relationship.

Plan the journey

Agree triggers, timing, content and staff handoff. Avoid repeating messages to a person who has already responded or opted out.

Measure meaningful response

Look at useful replies and progression through the sales process. Delivery and opening metrics do not establish revenue.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • How were contacts collected?
  • Which tools send the messages?
  • Who handles replies and requests to stop?
