What the engagement needs to solve

Won Ads designs CRM and marketing automation around defined triggers, data fields, owners and failure paths. The aim is to reduce repetitive work while keeping responsibility and customer communication clear.

Who this is for

Businesses with repeatable lead or customer processes that can be documented before tools are connected.

Problems we address

  • Leads copied manually between tools
  • No owner when an automation fails
  • Unclear pipeline stages and fields
  • Messages triggered without permission or context

What the scope can include

  • Process and data map
  • Platform and integration scope
  • Workflow build and testing
  • Documentation, training and support period

How performance is reviewed

Successful runs, assignment time, response time, data completeness, manual work reduced and downstream sales outcomes.

Working process

We diagnose the current position, agree the scope and responsibilities, implement in reviewable stages, validate the result and use evidence to decide the next improvement.

Before work begins

Final deliverables, access, exclusions, timelines, revision or optimisation cadence and third-party costs are confirmed in the proposal. Unsupported claims are not added.

Questions before you start

Which CRM platforms do you support?

Platform selection depends on the current stack and requirements. Specific supported tools must be confirmed during discovery.

Can every process be automated?

No. Unclear decisions and exceptional cases often need human ownership. We automate stable steps and document handoffs.

What access is required?

Access depends on the systems involved and should use least-privilege permissions wherever possible.

Do you test workflows before launch?

Yes. Test cases should cover expected journeys, missing data, duplicates, failed connections and manual recovery.

Is ongoing support included?

A defined support or optimisation period can be included. Duration and responsibilities are stated in the proposal.

A practical starting point

Keep inquiries moving through your workflow

Automation can connect lead capture, routing and follow-up. Define the human owner, permission requirements and failure path before building a workflow.

Map the manual process

Identify where inquiries arrive, which fields are required and who takes the next action. Fix unclear ownership before automating it.

Agree integration scope

Confirm supported tools, account permissions, software fees and data handling. Do not assume every platform exposes the same capabilities.

Test failure handling

Check duplicate inquiries, missing fields, provider errors and staff handoff. Keep a way to review failed steps instead of silently losing leads.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • Which CRM and channels are used?
  • Who owns each follow-up stage?
  • How are opt-outs handled?
