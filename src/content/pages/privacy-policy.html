How website inquiries are handled

This notice explains the information collected when you contact Won Ads through this website.

Information you choose to provide

Inquiry forms request contact details, business information, service interests and, in the detailed brief, budget and project requirements. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or confidential customer records.

Why the information is used

Won Ads uses inquiry details to understand your request, respond and discuss a possible project. Submitting a project inquiry is not an instruction to publish your details or a client testimonial.

Form delivery and external services

The forms use FormSubmit to transmit the information to wonads.com@gmail.com. The website also requests fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive information needed to process the request, such as network and browser information. Their own privacy notices apply to their processing.

FormSubmit privacy notice · Google privacy policy

Website measurement

If you choose Allow analytics, this website loads Google Analytics to measure visits and contact actions. Google receives network and device information and uses analytics cookies. Our contact events do not include form text, names, phone numbers or email addresses, and page URLs are sent without query strings or fragments. Advertising personalization is disabled. Choosing No thanks keeps optional analytics off. Your choice is saved in this browser; use Analytics preferences in the footer to change it. Withdrawing permission stops future measurement and removes first-party Google Analytics cookies.

Contact about your information

Email wonads.com@gmail.com to ask about information you supplied, request a correction or request deletion. Specify enough context to identify your inquiry without sending unnecessary sensitive information.
