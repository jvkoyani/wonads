Who this is for

Businesses with a clear offer, a workable response process and enough margin to test acquisition responsibly.

  • Campaign structure and launch plan

  • Optimisation and reporting cadence

Qualified enquiries, accepted opportunities, cost per qualified lead, conversion rate and sales feedback—not clicks alone.

Questions before you start

Is ad spend included in the management fee?

No. Media spend is paid to the advertising platform and is separate from Won Ads management fees. Your proposal confirms both responsibilities.

Who owns the advertising account?

The account and business data should remain under the client’s ownership, with the access required for Won Ads to manage the agreed work.

How much budget should a Chandigarh business start with?

The useful starting budget depends on demand, competition, sale value and the amount of testing required. We review these inputs before recommending a range.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Tracking can be included in scope. We first define the actions that represent a useful enquiry or sale, then validate the implementation.

Can you improve an existing campaign?

Yes. An account review can identify search-term, audience, creative, landing-page and measurement problems before a rebuild is proposed.

Do you guarantee leads or ROAS?

No responsible agency can guarantee a fixed result without controlling demand, offer, sales follow-up and market conditions. We agree measurable targets and report what the data supports.

Paid advertising connected to lead quality

Choose advertising channels around demand, offer readiness and the ability to follow up. Won Ads provides paid campaign services with media spend separate from management fees.

Search versus discovery

Google Search can reach people actively looking for a service. Meta campaigns can introduce an offer to an audience that is not searching for it yet. The better choice depends on the customer and the offer.

Before a campaign starts

Agree the offer, landing page, conversion action, geographic coverage and definition of an acceptable inquiry. Clarify account access and creative responsibilities.

After the click

Review unsuitable inquiries, sales feedback and conversion friction alongside advertising metrics. A cheap form submission is not automatically a useful business opportunity.

Questions to settle before commissioning the work

  • What is an acceptable customer acquisition cost?
  • Can your team respond to inquiries?
  • Who owns campaign accounts and creative?
