Choose marketing around how your customers buy

Local services, B2B services and ecommerce businesses need different acquisition and conversion plans. Use these questions to scope the work.

Local service businesses

Start with actual coverage, availability and a reliable inquiry route. A booking is only useful if your team can serve the customer. Explain exclusions and local availability on the website.

Local SEO · Lead funnels

B2B service businesses

Longer buying cycles need a clear offer, qualification and useful material for evaluation. Track sales stages rather than assuming every form submission is purchase-ready.

SEO · CRM workflows

Ecommerce businesses

A sales plan needs product margins, fulfilment capacity, return costs and repeat-purchase context. Advertising platform revenue alone cannot explain profitability.
