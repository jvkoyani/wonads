How to assess the fit

  • Share your offer, audience, service area and current challenge.
  • Agree what the project includes, who supplies assets and which access is required.
  • Separate deliverables from business outcomes that depend on budgets, competition and sales follow-up.
  • Ask for relevant work and permission-cleared evidence during the proposal discussion.

Founded by Suraj Jaiswal

Won Ads was founded by Suraj Jaiswal and brings four years of hands-on experience to brands in India and across international markets.
