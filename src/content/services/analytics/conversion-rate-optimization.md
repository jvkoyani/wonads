Conversion work starts by checking whether the right visitors understand the offer and can complete the next step. Research comes before a promise of conversion uplift.

### Check the basics

Test forms, mobile layouts, page speed and error messages. Fix broken steps before experimenting with button colours.

### Gather evidence

Use customer questions, usability observations and available analytics to form a specific hypothesis.

### Choose a suitable test

If traffic is limited, prioritize clear usability fixes and qualitative feedback. Do not claim statistical certainty from a handful of conversions.

### Questions to settle before commissioning the work

- Which action matters?
- Which page loses interested visitors?
- How much relevant traffic reaches it?
