Improve the connection between your business information, local service pages and customer inquiries. Accurate coverage and useful local evidence matter more than publishing a page for every nearby place.

### Business Profile eligibility

Confirm whether customers visit a staffed location or the business travels to customers. An online-only business cannot establish eligibility by adding a city to its website.

### Useful local content

Explain where the service is available, how customers contact the business and what they need to know before booking. Do not invent branches or duplicate city pages.

### Reviews and consistency

Ask actual customers for honest reviews without incentives or screening by satisfaction. Keep the business name, address and contact information consistent.

### Questions to settle before commissioning the work

- Is the address customer-facing?
- What is the real service area?
- Who owns the Business Profile?
