Meta advertising combines audience, offer and creative decisions. A test should explain what is changing and how lead or sales quality will be judged.

### Creative testing

Change a defined element such as the problem, proof or offer. Keep records so a result can inform the next creative decision.

### Form or website

An instant form can reduce steps; a landing page can explain a complex service in more depth. Evaluate the resulting inquiries, not just submission cost.

### Follow-up readiness

Confirm lead routing and the person responsible for replies before increasing spend.

### Questions to settle before commissioning the work

- Do you need new creative?
- How complex is the offer?
- How will sales outcomes be recorded?
