Search campaigns can reach people actively looking for a service. The work includes intent selection, geographic controls, relevant landing pages and a way to assess inquiry quality.

### Intent and exclusions

Separate research queries from buying queries. Review search terms and exclusions against the offer rather than relying only on click volume.

### Landing-page alignment

Repeat the actual service promise on the destination page, explain exclusions and make the inquiry step clear.

### Sales feedback

Track accepted inquiries and their qualification outcomes. Do not optimize only for cheap form fills.

### Questions to settle before commissioning the work

- What services should ads promote?
- What is the target geography?
- What makes an inquiry unsuitable?
